CodeAlpha Internship - Task 1: Market Research Report

Project Overview

This project was completed as part of the CodeAlpha Business & Marketing Strategy Internship. The report focuses on conducting market research for Swiggy, one of India’s leading food delivery platforms.

Task

Market Research Report

Selected Company

Swiggy

Objective

To analyze Swiggy's market position, target audience, competitors, and industry trends through market research.

Contents of the Report

* Company Overview
* SWOT Analysis
* Target Audience Analysis
* Competitor Analysis
* Market Trends
* Financial Performance (Sales & Profit/Loss)
* Findings and Recommendations

Tools Used

* Canva
* Google Research
* Microsoft Word

Deliverable

* Market Research Report (PDF)

[TASK 1 Report](TASK 1.pdf)

Key Learning Outcomes

* Market Research Techniques
* SWOT Analysis
* Competitor Analysis
* Understanding Consumer Behavior
* Business Strategy Evaluation

Conclusion

This report helped in understanding Swiggy’s business strategy, strengths, weaknesses, competitors, and market trends. It also provided insights into consumer behavior and the food delivery industry.
